DOCBOT 2026 Undergraduate Thesis · Explainable AI
BSc Undergrad Thesis ML Research Hierarchical Classification

DocBot 2026

An Explainable Framework for Preliminary Disease Prediction Using NLP Symptom Extraction and Hierarchical Machine Learning

Built a 771-disease taxonomy spanning 14 categories and 8 super-categories using Davidson's clinical grouping framework, then benchmarked four classification architectures against it — flat, 2-stage hierarchical, 3-stage hierarchical, and an adaptive hierarchical transfer approach. The 3-stage cascade is framed as the clinically interpretable option: explainability-first over raw accuracy.

Separately building a clinical validation layer: cross-referencing 5,372 disease–symptom pairs against two medical reference texts to ground the taxonomy in real clinical presentation categories and red-flag severity.

  • Four classification architectures, benchmarked head-to-head
  • 771-disease / 377-symptom hierarchical taxonomy
  • Explainability-first framing over raw accuracy
  • Two-textbook clinical cross-validation dataset
Python scikit-learn Hierarchical Classification NLP SHAP / XAI LaTeX
Supervised by Taofica Amrine, Asst. Professor · PCIU, Dept. of CSE
